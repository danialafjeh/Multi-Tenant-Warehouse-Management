Please read carefully before continuing :
Deleting your organization from Stockroom is a permanent action that cannot be undone.
* Permanent Data Loss : All data associated with your organization will be permanently deleted, including warehouses, products, categories, units of measurement, and inventory records.
* Stock Movement History : All stock movement records, including stock entries, stock exits, and transfers between warehouses, will be permanently deleted. You will no longer be able to review your organization's inventory history.
* Warehouse Information : All warehouses belonging to your organization will be deleted, along with their associated inventory data.
* Member Accounts : All user accounts belonging to members of your organization, including your own account, will be permanently deleted. You will no longer be able to access Stockroom using these accounts.
* Irreversible Action : Once the deletion process is completed, your organization's data cannot be recovered through Stockroom. Make sure you have saved any information you may need in the future.
* Organization Access : All members will immediately lose access to the organization and its associated resources. No member will be able to continue managing or viewing the organization's data.
* Historical Snapshots : Certain user snapshot records may remain in Stockroom for historical reference, even after the associated user accounts and organization have been deleted.
